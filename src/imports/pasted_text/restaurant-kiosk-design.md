Act as a Senior Product Designer (UX/UI), Restaurant Technology Consultant, and Design System Expert.

Your task is to design a world-class, premium, modern Restaurant Self-Service Kiosk System in Figma.

The final result should look like a product that could compete with McDonald's, KFC, Burger King, Wendy's, Shake Shack, Starbucks, and other global restaurant kiosk systems.

This is NOT a simple UI.
Design it as a commercial SaaS product that could be sold internationally.

==================================================
PROJECT
==================================================

Project Name:
Restaurant Self-Service Kiosk System

Goal:
Allow customers to browse the menu, customize meals, place orders, and pay without interacting with a cashier.

The design should focus on:

• Speed
• Simplicity
• Accessibility
• Premium Experience
• Modern UI
• Large touch-friendly components
• Clear navigation
• Beautiful food presentation
• Fast ordering flow

Everything must be optimized for large touch screens (21–32 inch kiosks).
